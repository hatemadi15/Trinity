# 30-second video storyboard — Earthrise

Global judges see the project page, the demo link and this video. No voice-over: captions only, so it reads with the sound off. Record the app at 1360×900 in a clean browser window (kiosk mode, no bookmarks bar), 1080p output, 30 fps, screen capture with OBS or the OS recorder; add captions and cuts in a simple editor. Two iterations are planned: v1 by 8 November, v2 after the rehearsals.

## Notes for the recording

- Use `?demo=1` so the assistant answers instantly from the rehearsed transcripts.
- Open Explore from the home page's de Gerlache Rim 2 place, so the period is 30 days from 1 January 2027 and the lander is the small default one; the Sun sets on 3 January and the long night after it is what shot 3 shows.
- For shot 4, add de Gerlache rim summit and de Gerlache Rim 2 to Compare beforehand (the ready pair "A ridge and the summit 12 km away" does it in one click).
- Keep the mouse out of frame between actions; move it only for the click that the caption describes.
- Captions: white on a dark band, one line, 44 px; hold each at least two seconds.
- Export at 1920×1080, H.264, under 50 MB; check it plays on a phone with the sound off.
