# 30-second video storyboard — Earthrise

Global judges see the project page, the demo link and this video. No voice-over: captions only, so it reads with the sound off. Record the app at 1360×900 in a clean browser window (kiosk mode, no bookmarks bar), 1080p output, 30 fps, screen capture with OBS or the OS recorder; add captions and cuts in a simple editor. Two iterations are planned: v1 by 8 November, v2 after the rehearsals.

| Time | Shot | On screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Black frame, white text | nothing else | "Fourteen days of night." |
| 3–8 s | Globe | `/explore` in globe mode; the Moon turns to the south pole, site pins appear, the camera settles on de Gerlache Rim 2 | "Every south-pole landing site lives or dies by two questions." |
| 8–15 s | Scrubber drag | Terrain mode; drag the date across January 2027; shadows sweep, the sky dome's Sun track skims the skyline, the timeline bars go dark, the verdict chip flips red | "When is the Sun up? When can Earth hear you?" |
| 15–21 s | Compare and best window | `/compare` with de Gerlache Rim 2 and Nobile Rim 2; click "Best 14-day window"; the columns turn green | "Compare sites. Find the window that keeps you lit and in contact." |
| 21–26 s | Planner and brief | Type "explain the longest night" in the planner strip; the answer appears with its source line; cut to `/brief` printing to one page | "Ask in plain words. Print the brief. Every number checked against JPL and LOLA." |
| 26–30 s | Logo card | "Earthrise" wordmark, then a line of data credits, then the URL | "NASA LRO LOLA. JPL DE440 and Horizons. Runs in your browser." then the live URL |

## Notes for the recording

- Use `?demo=1` so the planner answers instantly from the rehearsed transcripts.
- Set the start date to 2027-01-01 and 30 days before recording shot 3; the dark stretch in mid-January is what the caption points at.
- Keep the mouse out of frame between actions; move it only for the click that the caption describes.
- Captions: white on a dark band, one line, 44 px; hold each at least two seconds.
- Export at 1920×1080, H.264, under 50 MB; check it plays on a phone with the sound off.
