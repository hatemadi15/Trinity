# SDD ledger — part G: an easier player and a better Rocky (spec 21)

Branch claude/pensive-sagan-usqfey from main acdf9e3 (part F and the Node.js notes merged), with Rocky's night fix (a0f5623) first. The user's asks of 12 November, two days before going live: the player (the time bar) more intuitive and less clunky, as it has too much on it, is hard to scrub and does not say what to do, with a speed for Play; and Rocky better designed: cuter, easier to spot, more alien, and doing something when found. Nothing may cost frame time at rest or build a shader program.

Pre-flight rulings:
- The player's changes came from a critique of the bar in use (a subagent drove the production build in Playwright at 1440 × 900, 768 × 1024 and 390 × 844 with touch, and timed Play, the holds of a moment's line and the taps on the phone's chip); its seven changes for the bar and three for the stand view's row, ranked by the clunk each removes per hour, stood for a plan.
- Next is a fixed button beside Play, not a chip that moves along the track: a month is five presses of the same place, and the review's own finding was a chip that looked like a button and was not one.
- Rocky is seen in the cloud through SwiftShader (the stand view's own renderer, a studio page that puts the camera anywhere, and the production build found by turning, by day and at night); frame rates there mean nothing, programs and draws are real.

The groups, as landed:
- Rocky (d5fae49): a dome made as the rocks are (`rockDome`) in a boulder's material, stubby round feet, a big round head, big eyes with dark pupils and white catchlights, two feelers with sea-green tips; the eyes smoother than the rocks and faintly lit from within, the tips more, every glow under the bloom's threshold; three quarters on, head down at the dust, and on being found it turns its head to look at you in 1.1 s (at once with reduced motion), frames only while it turns (counted in the studio: five frames in 1.1 s, one sharp frame 0.3 s after, then none). 29 programs in the rendered stand view with and without it, as before; found by day after 44 to 52 presses of the arrow key and at night after 44 to 46. Ruling: the shell is no longer a boulder's own geometry (the brief's flat test scene drew it as a brick from an angular block), but a dome made by the rocks' own surface code, so it still reads as one of them; a few hundred corners are uploaded once, no program built.
- The view keeps up with Play at any speed (8edc225): `glideStep` glides at 7/6 of Play's pace, so each of its 12 steps a second is caught up within its own twelfth of a second; beyond two steps, a jump.
- What to watch names Play and Next (23d26e8): one line at every width, as the dots and Next are the same at every width.
- The bar as a player (d53bc88, a subagent in its own worktree, owning the bar's three files): `PLAY_HPS` 24 with 1×, 2× and 4×; holds on each moment (`holdMs`, `onArrive`); Play gives way to the hand, Space plays (`spaceToPlay`), Replay at the end; the ink fill; Next with Shift and the arrows; dots at every width (the flags' rows, stems, width measuring and the phone's chip gone, `dotShifts` kept); a 47 px target over the lanes and a readout of the hour with the lanes' words (`onHover`); the key behind "Key ▾" on a phone. 78 tests in its file.
